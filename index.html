          Disaster information and safety guide

          Typhoon

          A tropical storm with strong winds and heavy rain that can cause floods and landslides.

          Before: Pack your go-bag, secure windows and roofing, charge your phone, and know your evacuation center.

          During: Stay indoors away from windows. Follow official alerts. Evacuate if told to.

          After: Avoid floodwater and fallen wires. Check on neighbors and report damage.

          Flood

          Water rising over normal ground level, often after heavy rain or when rivers overflow.

          Before: Know high ground nearby. Keep documents in waterproof bags.

          During: Move to higher ground. Turn off electricity. Never walk or drive through floodwater.

          After: Boil drinking water if unsure it is safe. Clean and dry your home.

          Earthquake

          Sudden shaking of the ground. Aftershocks can follow.

          Before: Secure heavy furniture. Plan a meeting point.

          During: Drop, cover, and hold on. Stay away from glass and shelves. If outside, move to an open area.

          After: Expect aftershocks. Check for injuries and gas leaks. Leave damaged buildings.

          Fire

          An uncontrolled fire in a home or building that spreads quickly.

          Before: Check wiring and stoves. Know two ways out of every room.

          During: Get out and stay out. Crawl low under smoke. Call 911 once you are safe.

          After: Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe.

          Landslide

          Soil and rocks sliding down a slope, often after long rain.

          Before: Watch for cracks in the ground and tilting trees. Know your evacuation route.

          During: Move away from the slide path, uphill sideways if possible.

          After: Stay away from the slope. Report broken utilities.
